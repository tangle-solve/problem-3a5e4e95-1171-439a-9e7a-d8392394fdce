### CHEM-000417: Predicting methyl-iodide capture by chemically aged silver mordenite
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Predict breakthrough from measured populations of metallic silver, exchanged Ag(I), and nitrate species after variable NOₓ/moisture histories. Account for iodine capture by each population.
- **Status:** A 2020 study identified oxidation and inward silver migration, with nitrates carrying up to 95% of capacity in well-aged samples. A 2024 study adds moisture and storage effects; prospective state-to-breakthrough prediction remains open.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Replaces fresh-sorbent capacity assumptions with chemically informed lifetime prediction.
- **Key works:** H. Goettsche et al. (2024), “Effects of moisture and aging upon decomposition of methyl iodide by reduced silver mordenite,” [DOI: 10.1007/s10450-024-00473-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10450-024-00473-8); A. I. Wiechert et al. (2020), “Capture of Iodine from Nuclear-Fuel-Reprocessing Off-Gas: Influence of Aging on a Reduced Silver Mordenite Adsorbent after Exposure to NO/NO2,” [DOI: 10.1021/acsami.0c15456](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.0c15456).
- **Related:** Reliability engineering—chemically state-dependent adsorbent breakthrough.
